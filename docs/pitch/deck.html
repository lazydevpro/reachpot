The problem

Paying creators per view is broken.

Brands already pay "clippers" to post Shorts about them. Fixed-CPM programs fail creators in three ways:

First-come drain

Budgets run out early. Late creators with great posts earn nothing.

Opaque payouts

The platform decides what counts and pays off-chain, when and where it likes.

Rejection pays

Rejecting a submission saves the brand money, so creators carry all the risk.

And on Portaldot: new projects need distribution and real utility for their tokens, and creators need a way to turn a project token into something they can use.

The solution

A pot, escrowed on-chain, split by views.

A brand locks a pot in any Portaldot token. Creators post Shorts. When the window closes, the pot splits pro-rata by views, with a per-creator cap, and every creator is paid on-chain.

100%

of the pot is spent. A capped creator's excess re-splits across everyone else ("water-filling").

0

ways for the brand to claw back a live pot. Only the never-allocated remainder is refundable.

1 tap

for a creator to cash a project-token payout out to POT on Portaldot's native DEX.

Why Portaldot is essential

Native modules and EVM, working as one product.

Revive EVM + native-asset ERC-20 precompilesReachpot.sol escrows native Assets tokens directly — any project token works, no wrapping, no bridge.
H160 ↔ native account mappingBrands fund from MetaMask; creators use a Portaldot wallet; after revive.mapAccount, EVM payouts land in their native account.
AssetConversion (native DEX)Creators swap payouts to POT in one tap with live on-chain quotes; campaigns add volume to the token's pool.
TransactionStorageThe allocation snapshot is published on-chain; the contract pins its hash and location so anyone can re-derive the root.
NftsSoulbound creator badges whose attributes (campaigns, views, earned) are a portable on-chain résumé.
On a plain EVM chain this needs a separate DEX, NFT contract and data-availability layer.

Business model & growth

A growth engine for Portaldot projects.

Who pays

Portaldot projects launching tokens, and Web2 brands running creator campaigns. They fund pots; creators earn.

Revenue (planned)

A small protocol fee on each pot, verified-analytics tiers for brands, and featured campaign placement.

The loop

A project funds a pot in its own token → creators post and get paid → creators cash out on the native DEX → the token gains liquidity and holders, and Portaldot gains users who arrived through a Short.

Why it wins

Fair by construction (pro-rata, capped, no claw-back), verifiable by anyone, and paid in seconds instead of net-30.
